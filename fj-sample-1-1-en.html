10 Source and method of pricing

  1. Quantities from statistical ratiosUsable area × quantity per m² collected from real 2-storey houses
  2. Unit prices at current Phuket levelLocal real prices first → Provincial Commerce Office prices → Reference price base adjusted for year and area
  3. Summed by categoryAdd 5% contingency for direct cost, then contractor overhead + profit 12%
  4. Cross-checked 2 waysWork-group shares vs range, and cost per m² vs statistics
